Ford Bronco Roll Bar Fitment Guide — 2021 Through 2026, All Trims

The 2021–2026 full-size Ford Bronco uses a consistent structural roll cage design that has not changed between the first production year and the current model year. The cage tube dimensions — approximately 1.5 to 1.75 inches outer diameter on the A-pillar, B-pillar, and rear sport bar sections — are identical across all nine factory trim levels. This means an accessory that fits a 2021 Base Bronco also fits a 2026 Raptor without modification, with the exception of overhead MOLLE panels that differ between 2-door and 4-door body styles.

It does not share any cage tube diameter, mounting point, or accessory ecosystem with the full-size model. Everything on this page applies exclusively to the full-size 2021–2026 Ford Bronco in 2-door and 4-door body styles only.

Everglades adds factory snorkel. Heritage Edition launched 2023. Roll cage dimensions unchanged from 2021. All five accessories on this page confirmed compatible with 2023 and 2024 model years without modification.

How to Choose Ford Bronco Roll Bar Accessories — Buyer’s Guide

Not every Bronco owner needs all five accessories at once. Here’s how to prioritize based on how you use your Bronco.

Grab Handle Priority: Always Install These First

Factory Bronco grab handles are inadequate for any real off-road use. If you trail-drive your Bronco even occasionally, replacing the plastic factory handles with paracord-wrapped metal clamp handles is the highest-impact safety upgrade you can make for under $75. Passengers without a solid grab handle in rough terrain are at genuine risk of injury from contact with the cage bars you’re about to pad. Install grab handles before anything else on this list.

Overhead MOLLE Panel: Best for Gear-Heavy Trail Use

If you run first aid, comms, navigation, or trail recovery equipment inside the Bronco, an overhead MOLLE panel is the cleanest organizational solution available. Gear that slides on the rear seat cushion during a traverse, or gets thrown across the cargo area on a sudden stop, stays exactly where you put it when it’s mounted to a ceiling MOLLE grid. The key decision point is door count — order the 2-door or 4-door SKU that matches your Bronco. If you have Bartact seat covers, the seatback MOLLE panel extends the overhead panel’s usable mounting area down through the entire height of the front seat.

Chase Rack: For Recovery Gear That Shouldn’t Ride Inside

Recovery gear is dirty, greasy, and heavy. A Hi-Lift jack can contaminate the entire cargo area with hydraulic fluid from even a small valve leak. Maxtrax boards pick up mud and gravel that abrades seat and carpet materials over dozens of trail runs. A sport bar chase rack solves this by relocating exterior recovery gear to the rear sport bars — accessible without opening the tailgate, and contained outside the passenger compartment entirely. For Bronco owners who run regular trail recovery gear, a chase rack pays back its cost in cargo area cleanliness within a season.

Roll Bar Padding: Safety Investment with Low Cost and Low Complexity

At under $30 for a full tube, pre-slit FMVSS 302 foam padding is the cheapest item on this list and arguably the most important safety item in a roll bar build. Exposed cage bars at head height are a hazard on technical terrain, particularly during rock crawling, water crossings with sudden drops, or any situation where occupants could make sudden lateral contact with the cage. Fire-retardant foam absorbs impact energy and — critically — will not add fuel to a post-incident fire. This is a 10-minute install with no tools and no excuses not to do it.

5-Step Roll Bar Accessory Installation Sequence

Installing roll bar accessories in the right order prevents you from having to remove and reinstall components. Follow this sequence for the cleanest install with the least rework.

  1. Seat covers first. Install Bartact front seat covers before any overhead work. With front seats positioned forward and headrests removed, you have clean access to the seat top and rear seatback. Connect the MOLLE seatback panel attachment points per Bartact’s instructions. Reinstall headrests. The Bartact install creates the base of your MOLLE system.
  2. Roll bar padding second. Snap FMVSS 302 foam padding onto all exposed A-pillar and B-pillar bar sections at head height before the overhead panel goes in. Once the overhead panel is installed, access to the upper cage sections is restricted. Pad first, panel second.
  3. Overhead MOLLE panel third. With padding in place, bolt the Hooke Road overhead panel to the existing cage mounting points. Torque to spec — typically 8 to 12 ft-lbs on cage hardware. Do not overtorque cage fasteners. The panel will sit flush against the padded cage bars when correctly installed.
  4. Grab handles fourth. Clamp handles to A-pillar and B-pillar bars at positions ergonomically reachable by average-height occupants seated normally in your Bronco. Front passenger handle should be at the same reach distance as the driver’s grab point. Rear handles should be reachable from the rear seat without leaning. Tighten clamps firmly but do not mar the cage finish.
  5. Chase rack last. With all interior work complete, mount the CLAMBER chase rack to the rear sport bars. Load-test the rack by hand before attaching any recovery gear — physically pull and shake the rack in multiple directions to confirm it is secure. Attach gear in order of weight: heaviest items (Hi-Lift, jerry cans) closest to the cage, lightest items (folding shovels, bungee storage) outward.

Frequently Asked Questions — Ford Bronco Roll Bar Accessories

What roll bars does the Ford Bronco come with from the factory?
Every 2021–2026 full-size Bronco ships with a complete structural steel roll cage including A-pillar bars, B-pillar bars, and rear sport bars. Factory grab handles are lightweight plastic A-pillar units. The overhead cage space is empty. All aftermarket roll bar accessories on this page are upgrades the Bronco does not include from the factory.

Do Ford Bronco roll bar accessories fit both 2-door and 4-door models?
Grab handles, roll bar padding, and chase racks are universal between body styles. Overhead MOLLE panels are door-count specific because the interior cage length differs between 2-door and 4-door Broncos. Always confirm door count when ordering overhead cage-mounted panels.

What is the cage tube diameter on the Ford Bronco?
The Bronco’s factory cage uses approximately 1.5- to 1.75-inch outer diameter tubing across the main A-pillar, B-pillar, and rear sport bar sections. Most aftermarket clamp-based accessories designed for this range will fit without modification. Verify the product’s listed clamp range covers 1.5 to 1.75 inches before ordering.

Are Bartact seat covers compatible with the Ford Bronco’s roll cage layout?
Yes. Bartact Bronco tactical seat covers feature a MOLLE seatback panel engineered to connect with overhead MOLLE panels mounted to the roll cage bars — creating a continuous gear storage system from seat to ceiling. Custom-fit for 2021–2026, airbag-compatible on all trims, made in the USA from 1000D Cordura.

What fire-retardant roll bar padding works on the Ford Bronco?
Pre-slit foam padding with 1.5- to 1.75-inch inner diameter fits the Bronco’s cage bars. Oodles of Noodles fire-retardant foam meets FMVSS 302 standards and snaps on without adhesive. Focus coverage on A-pillar and B-pillar bars at head and shoulder height.

Can I add a sport bar chase rack to the Ford Bronco without drilling?
Yes. Clamp-based chase racks like the CLAMBER model attach to the rear sport bars using an adjustable bracket sized for 1.5- to 2-inch tubing — no drilling, no body modification. Verify clearance with factory swing-out spare carriers before mounting.

Does the Bronco Sport use the same roll bar accessories as the full-size Bronco?
No. The Bronco Sport is an unrelated unibody crossover with no equivalent structural cage. All products on this page are for the full-size 2021–2026 Ford Bronco only.

How do I build a complete Ford Bronco roll bar accessory system?
Build in five layers: (1) Bartact MOLLE seat covers as your interior base, (2) paracord grab handles on all cage bar positions, (3) overhead MOLLE panel on the cage bars above the rear seats, (4) sport bar chase rack on the rear sport bars for exterior recovery carry, (5) FMVSS 302 foam padding on all exposed cage sections at head height. This five-layer system uses every inch of the Bronco’s structural cage as a functional, organized trail platform.
