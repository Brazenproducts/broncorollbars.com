Best Ford Bronco Roll Bar Accessories 2026

The factory roll bars on the 2021–2026 Bronco are structural steel — well built, but bare. These are the grab handles, overhead MOLLE panels, foam padding, and sport bar chase racks that off-road owners actually run on trail. Ranked and reviewed honestly.

Updated: August 2026  ·  Full-size Bronco 2-door and 4-door  ·  No Bronco Sport

The Ford Bronco's roll cage gives you a lot to work with — exposed A-pillar bars, B-pillar bars, overhead bars above the rear seats, and the rear sport bars behind row two. Every one of those surfaces is either a grab point, a mounting rail, or a safety hazard depending on what you do with them. This page covers the best aftermarket options for all of it.

We separate the premium domestic options (Bartact, Bull Strap) from the Amazon aftermarket. Both have a place. The premium brands cost more and earn it. The Amazon options are solid for budget builds or for covering bars the premium brands don't make dedicated products for yet.

Full-size Bronco only. These products are tested and confirmed for the 2021–2026 Ford Bronco 2-door and 4-door. None of this applies to the Bronco Sport, which has a different interior and no exposed roll cage bars.

What You're Actually Working With

The 2021–2026 Ford Bronco full-size ships with a complete steel roll cage — not a cosmetic bar, a real structural cage. The A-pillars, B-pillars, and rear sport bars are all exposed on the interior when you're running a soft top or no top. That bare steel is your canvas for aftermarket work.

The factory grab handles on the A-pillars are small plastic units — adequate for on-road use, inadequate for anything aggressive off-road where a passenger needs a real grip during a side hill or a rock crawl. Every option on this list addresses that gap in a different way.

Grab Handles: Premium vs. Amazon

Bartact and Bull Strap both make custom-fit Bronco grab handles. "Custom-fit" matters here — the clamp is machined to the Bronco's roll bar tube diameter, not a generic collet trying to fit every 1.5–2" tube on the market. The paracord wrap is also purpose-chosen: it grips through gloves, doesn't get slick when wet, and won't tear under a 250-pound load on a hard jolt.

The SEVEN SPARTA Amazon set is a legitimate alternative. The fitment isn't quite as precision-matched but it works well, covers all four grab points at once, and ships fast. For an $80–100 full-set upgrade it's hard to argue with the reviews. For people who run their Bronco hard on technical trail, the premium brands earn their price.

Overhead MOLLE Panels: Underused Real Estate

Most Bronco owners never think about the space directly above the rear seats. Once you install an overhead MOLLE panel on the roll cage bars up there, you immediately wonder how you went without it. Gear bags, a first-aid kit, a radio pouch, a knife — all of it goes up and out of the seat area. The Hooke Road panel is the most commonly seen option in this category; PTYYDS is the closest alternative when stock is limited.

Roll Bar Padding: The Safety Upgrade Nobody Does

People add grab handles for comfort and remove their roof for the open-air experience. They rarely think about what happens when a passenger's head meets a steel A-pillar bar on a hard jolt. Pre-slit foam padding is a $15–25 fix that covers the bars at head height and meets fire retardant standards. Install it in ten minutes. It's not dramatic, but it's the upgrade most likely to matter on the day you actually need it.

FAQ

What are the best grab handles for the Ford Bronco roll bars?
Bartact and Bull Strap make the best Bronco grab handles — custom-fit paracord wraps for the 2021–2026 full-size Bronco's roll bar tubing. Both are made to fit, not to fit-most. On Amazon, the SEVEN SPARTA 4-pack is the top-rated option for a full four-door upgrade in a single purchase.
Do overhead MOLLE panels attach to Bronco roll bars?
Yes. The Hooke Road and PTYYDS overhead MOLLE panels bolt to the roll cage bars above the rear seats using existing mount points — no drilling. They create overhead MOLLE storage for gear bags, radios, first aid kits, and trail essentials.
What foam padding fits Ford Bronco roll bars?
Pre-slit foam in the 1.5–1.75-inch range fits the Bronco cage bars. The Oodles of Noodles fire-retardant foam clamps on without adhesive, meets FMVSS 302 standards, and covers exposed A-pillar and B-pillar bars at head height.
Do these accessories fit both 2-door and 4-door Broncos?
Most do. Bartact and Bull Strap grab handles are available in both configurations. The SEVEN SPARTA 4-pack covers all four grab points on both body styles. Always verify your door count and model year in the listing before ordering.
Does any of this work on the Bronco Sport?
No. The Bronco Sport has a completely different interior structure and no exposed roll cage bars. Everything on this page is for the 2021–2026 Ford Bronco full-size (2-door and 4-door) only.
